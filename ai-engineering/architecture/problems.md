# Problems

A system can look distributed and still behave like one monolith. This page is the signs of that coupling: shared queues, databases, storage, and messaging.

[6 symptoms of a distributed monolith](https://torvo.com.au/articles/6-symptoms-of-a-distributed-monolith): in general, coupling of services, deployments, versions, and dependencies, and sharing the same queues, databases, storage, and messaging systems, by Ben Brazier.
