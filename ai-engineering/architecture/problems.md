# Problems

A system can look distributed and still behave like one monolith. This page names the signs of that coupling, so they can be spotted before the services are split any further.

The signs are listed in [6 symptoms of a distributed monolith](https://torvo.com.au/articles/6-symptoms-of-a-distributed-monolith): in general, coupling of services, deployments, versions, and dependencies, and sharing the same queues, databases, storage, and messaging systems, by Ben Brazier.
