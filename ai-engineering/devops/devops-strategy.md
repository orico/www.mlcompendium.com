# DevOps Strategy

Delivery speed without a strategy stalls. DevOps combines IT operations and software development so applications ship accurately and quickly, and this page points at the strategies an organization can adopt to get there.

The same notes are in [Definitions](../definitions.md).

The one source here is marked (good): [8 DevOps strategies and how to implement them](https://www.spiceworks.com/tech/devops/articles/devops-strategies-to-test/) is the Spiceworks article on the key DevOps strategies organizations can adopt and how to put each one in place.
