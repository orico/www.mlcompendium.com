# Logs

Production systems leave a trail, and that trail is only useful if someone can collect and analyze it. This page covers logging through Logz.io, first what the product is and then how log analysis works in it.

The same notes are in [ELK](elk.md).

## Logz.io

Logz.io has two short videos that do this in order. [Intro](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LqJYeeTss9Q) is Introducing Logz.io, and [What is](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6VVig5tnTJE) is Log Analysis with Logz.io, both from Logz.io itself.
