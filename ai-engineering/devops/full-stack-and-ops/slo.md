# SLO

Uptime promises need measurable objectives, and writing those objectives by hand for every service is tedious. This page points at Sloth, a generator for Prometheus service level objectives.

[Sloth](https://itnext.io/slos-should-be-easy-say-hi-to-sloth-9c8a225df0d4), [GitHub](https://github.com/slok/sloth) are the announcement post, "SLOs should be easy, say hi to Sloth", which starts from SLOs as one of the buzz words of the year, and the slok/sloth repo itself. The repo describes the tool in one line:

> Easy and simple Prometheus SLO (service level objectives) generator
