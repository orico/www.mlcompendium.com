# MLOps Intro

ML in a notebook is not ML in production. This page is the building blocks, reading list, Analytics Vidhya guides, and the without-Ops series for that gap.

The same notes are in [Definitions](../definitions.md) and [Life cycle](../../business-problems/data-science.md#life-cycle).
