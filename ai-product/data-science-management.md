# Data Science Management

A data science team still has to hire, interview, write, and stay legal. This page is recruiting, interview questions, politics, docs, contracts, due diligence, and general notes.

## Hiring / recruiting

 2. SQL
 3. Database design
 4. Data architecture/big data technologies
 5. Soft skills
