# Data Science Management

A data science team still has to hire, interview, write, and stay legal. The page follows that order: recruiting and interview questions first, then the politics and design docs of the daily work, then contracts and due diligence, and finally general notes on keeping data scientists effective.

## Hiring / recruiting

A team starts with a hire, and the first thing to settle is which skills you are hiring for. [Data engineer skills](https://medium.com/@m_mcclarty/data-engineering-interview-guide-7a14d10887dd) is a brief data engineering interview prep guide, written by a data engineer of three years who noticed a distinct lack of readily available resources, partly because the role varies from company to company and domain to domain. The skills it expects are:

 1. Coding (Typically Python)
 2. SQL
 3. Database design
 4. Data architecture/big data technologies
 5. Soft skills

## Interview questions

Once the skills are named, the interview has to test them. These question banks cover ensembles, trees, and k-NNs.

The same notes are in [Decision Trees](../predictive-ml/decision-trees.md), [Ensembles](../predictive-ml/ensembles.md), and [KNN](../predictive-ml/nearest-neighbors.md#knn).

[40 questions on ensembles](https://www.analyticsvidhya.com/blog/2017/02/40-questions-to-ask-a-data-scientist-on-ensemble-modeling-techniques-skilltest-solution/?utm_source=facebook.com&utm_medium=social) is Ankit Gupta's set of questions on ensemble modeling techniques to test a data scientist, covering bagging, boosting, stacking, and voting. [30 on trees](https://www.analyticsvidhya.com/blog/2017/09/30-questions-test-tree-based-models/?utm_source=facebook.com&utm_medium=social) is his companion set on tree based models, including decision trees, random forest, and boosting algorithms. [30 on knns](https://www.analyticsvidhya.com/blog/2017/09/30-questions-test-k-nearest-neighbors-algorithm/?utm_source=facebook.com&utm_medium=social) is Sunil Ray's KNN interview questions for freshers and mid-senior professionals, with explanations and solutions.

## Politics

Interviews are not the hard part once the team is in place. [The most difficult thing in ds, politics](https://medium.com/data-science/the-most-difficult-thing-in-data-science-politics-ca6dd781da56) is the post "The most difficult thing in data science: politics", originally published on rDisorder.

## Writing docs

Politics is easier when decisions are written down. [Design docs at google](https://www.industrialempathy.com/posts/design-docs-at-google/) describes design docs as one of the key elements of how Google builds software.

## Legal & contracts

Docs are not contracts, and advisors need one. [(FAST) Advisory board saas agreement](https://fi.co/fast) is the FAST Agreement, used by tens of thousands of entrepreneurs and advisors per year to establish productive working relationships, trading advice and support for a standardized amount of equity.

## Due Diligence

Contracts sit beside diligence, where someone outside checks whether the ML is real. The guide [by Inbal Budowski Tal](https://inbal-budo.medium.com/how-to-run-a-machine-learning-technical-due-diligence-415970f77d5f) is how to run a machine learning technical due diligence for VCs, estimating the depth of a startup's ML technology and the team's capability to deliver their "claim" and win the "fame".

## General

After hiring, docs, and contracts, the last question is how to keep data scientists effective once they are there.

1. [The Care and Feeding of Data Scientists - O'Reilly](https://oreilly-ds-report.s3.amazonaws.com/Care_and_Feeding_of_Data_Scientists.pdf) is the O'Reilly report on that question.

[Transitioning From AI Teams To AI Squads](https://pub.towardsai.net/transitioning-from-ai-teams-to-ai-squads-38696e0ec6b4) (October 2024) is the team shape on this page.
[Setting Up Data Science Teams For Success](https://pub.towardsai.net/setting-up-data-science-teams-for-success-b1bee9765517) (October 2022) is the team-setup note, built around Dr. Ori Cohen's three pillars of data science, and the data-team page is the engineering side of the same idea.
