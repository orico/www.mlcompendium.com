# Growth

A product has to grow after it ships. The page first defines product-led growth and how usage and retention are driven inside the product, then looks at a growth tool whose claims were not validated here.

The same notes are in [Business](business.md), [Product Vision & Strategy](product-vision-and-strategy.md), and [User Experience Design (UX)](user-experience-design-ux.md).

The idea starts with a definition. [Productled.com](https://productled.com/blog/product-led-growth-definition/) — definition and why it is taking off. The practice behind it is [Mixpanel with Ken Rudin](https://mixpanel.com/blog/googles-head-of-user-growth-on-driving-product-usage-and-retention/) on "Google’s head of user growth on driving product usage and retention", which comes down to two moves. The first is raising feature awareness — through UI changes. Identifying and increasing the adoption of these features is very often a great way to accelerate product growth. The second is promoting a feature **in context** to drive usage and retention, i.e., when the user needs it.

Those moves only work when the product itself is the sales channel. [A guide to product-led growth](https://www.trychameleon.com/blog/product-led-growth) defines product-led growth (PLG) as users experiencing product value through free trials or freemium plans, having your product sell itself, with examples and a framework.
