# Product / Program Managers

Someone has to own the product and the program. The page points first at the product side, the AI product manager, and then at the program side, the data program manager.

The same notes are in [Project Management](management.md#project-management).

The product owner comes first, because an AI product fails differently from ordinary software. [the AI PM](https://emerj.com/ai-executive-guides/ai-product-manager/) is written for someone who finds themselves in the role of an AI product manager, and covers what it takes to deliver a successful AI product at a company, a role the guide calls key for the future of AI deployment.

Once the product has an owner, the data work around it still needs someone to run the program. [The Data Program Manager](http://www.ddmcd.com/dpm-intro) is an introduction to Data Program Management (DPM) from www.ddmcd.com.
