# Product Vision & Strategy

A product needs a direction before a backlog. The page goes from vision to strategy and roadmap, then to how a strategy is built, and ends with how the product is positioned against others.

The same notes are in [Growth](growth.md).

The chain starts with vision, strategy, and roadmap, and a strategy is usually one of 4 strategy types: cost, differentiation, focus, or quality. [what is?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebwo_BX_VtU) (youtube) walks the full ladder of mission, vision, strategy, roadmap, and execution from a product strategist's point of view. The talk that connects vision to delivery is "[Product Strategy: The Missing Link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x4H_gluZI10)" by Inspired Author Marty Cagan of SVPG at Lean Product Meetup, on youtube.

Once the ladder is clear, the strategy itself has to be built. [Strategy should be aligned with Vision](https://www.hotjar.com/product-strategy/) is a complete guide to what a product strategy is, why it matters, the key elements of top strategies, and how to build one yourself.

A strategy then has to say where the product stands in the market. [product positioning strategies](https://www.shopkick.com/partners/blog/3-types-of-product-positioning-strategies-and-how-to-best-leverage-them-fc) names three: comparative, differentiation, and segmentation.
