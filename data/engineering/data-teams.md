# Data Teams

Analytics only lands if the team shape can ship it. Once the vision is written, the page points at how to build an analytics team and then at how to set a data science team up for success.

The same notes are in [Building Data/DS teams](../../business-problems/data-science.md#building-datads-teams), [Building Teams](../../ai-product/management.md#building-teams), and [Data Vision](data-vision.md).

[How to build an effective analytics team](https://www.analytics8.com/blog/how-to-build-an-effective-data-analytics-team/) is Kevin Lobo's best practices for building and optimizing a high-impact data analytics team, with tips on being a successful data leader.

The analytics team is one shape; the data science team needs its own foundations. See also [Setting Up Data Science Teams For Success](https://pub.towardsai.net/setting-up-data-science-teams-for-success-b1bee9765517) (October 2022), built around a figure of the three pillars of data science.
