# DOE Tools

Once an experiment is planned, something has to generate the design table. This page is that tooling rather than the theory itself: two generators from the same author first, then the pyDOE factorial docs, then a worked factorial notebook.

[Design of experiments](https://github.com/tirthajyoti/Design-of-experiment-Python) is tirthajyoti/Design-of-experiment-Python on GitHub, a design-of-experiment (DOE) generator for science, engineering, and statistics. Its packaged successor is [doePy](https://github.com/tirthajyoti/doepy), tirthajyoti/doepy, the Design of Experiment Generator; read the docs at: https://doepy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/.

For the classic factorial layouts, [PyDoe](https://pythonhosted.org/pyDOE/factorial.html) is the Factorial Designs page of the pyDOE 0.3.6 documentation. To see one of those layouts worked end to end, the [DOE Notebook](https://charlesreid1.github.io/empirical-model-building/factorial_2level_3factor.html#twolevelfactorial) is the Factorial - Two-Level Three-Factor Design.
