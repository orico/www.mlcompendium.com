# Hypothesis Testing

Every experiment ends in a decision, and hypothesis testing is the statistical language behind it. The page first explains the idea, then runs a two-sample test in Python. The same notes are in [A/B Testing](a-b-testing.md), [Mann-Whitney U test](../data/distribution-transformation.md#mann-whitney-u-test), and [Null hypothesis](../data/distribution-transformation.md#null-hypothesis).

The idea comes first. (good) [HT explained](https://medium.com/data-science/hypothesis-testing-explained-how-i-wish-it-was-explained-to-me-14379f7a41c3) is Hypothesis Testing Explained (How I Wish It Was Explained to Me), written by someone who first learned it in the first year of a Bachelor's in Statistics and always felt something about it was missing. The same (good) HT explained article also sits at its Towards Data Science address, [https://towardsdatascience.com/hypothesis-testing-explained-how-i-wish-it-was-explained-to-me-14379f7a41c3](https://towardsdatascience.com/hypothesis-testing-explained-how-i-wish-it-was-explained-to-me-14379f7a41c3), the publication that calls itself the home for data science and AI.

With the idea clear, [two sample HT with python](https://levelup.gitconnected.com/two-sample-hypothesis-tests-with-python-43e1b8c52306) is the two-sample test run in code.
