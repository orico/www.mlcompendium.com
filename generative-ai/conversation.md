# Conversation

This page points at Cornell ConvoKit and its conversational analysis features.
ConvoKit ships conversational datasets plus feature extractors such as politeness strategies.

1. [ConvoKit](https://github.com/CornellNLP/ConvoKit) by Cornell — is a toolkit for extracting conversational features and analyzing social phenomena in conversations. It includes several large conversational datasets along with scripts exemplifying the use of the toolkit on these datasets.
 - Research on a computational approach to politeness with application to social factors - Cristian Danescu-Niculescu-Mizil. #### [Politeness strategies](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~cristian/Politeness.html)
 - politenessStrategies — convokit 4.1.2 documentation. [(API)](https://convokit.cornell.edu/documentation/politenessStrategies.html)

 This note is about lexical and parse-based features that correlate with politeness.

 A set of lexical and parse-based features correlating with politeness and impoliteness.
 - tisjune.github.io. tisjune.github.io. [Expected Conversational Context Framework](https://tisjune.github.io/research/dissertation)
 - Expected Context Framework — convokit 4.1.2 documentation. [(API)](https://convokit.cornell.edu/documentation/expected_context_model.html)

 A framework for characterizing utterances and terms based on their expected conversational context, consisting of model implementations and wrapper pipelines.
 - Research on characterizing online public discussions through patterns of participant interactions - Cristian Danescu-Niculescu-Mizil. #### [Hypergraph conversation representation](http://www.cs.cornell.edu/~cristian/Patterns_of_participant_interactions.html)
 - Hyperconvo — convokit 4.1.2 documentation. [(API)](https://convokit.cornell.edu/documentation/hyperconvo.html)

 This note is about representing conversations as hypergraphs to extract structural features.

 A method for extracting structural features of conversations through a hypergraph representation.
 - Research on finding your voice: the linguistic development of mental health counselors - Cristian Danescu-Niculescu-Mizil. #### [Linguistic diversity in conversations](http://www.cs.cornell.edu/~cristian/Finding_your_voice__linguistic_development.html)
 - Speaker Convo Diversity — convokit 4.1.2 documentation. [(API)](https://convokit.cornell.edu/documentation/speakerConvoDiversity.html)

 This note measures how diverse a speaker’s language is within and across conversations.

 A method to compute the linguistic diversity of individuals within their own conversations, and between other individuals in a population.
