# Federated Learning

Unlearning removes data after training; federated learning keeps the data where it lives and brings the training to it. This short page points to one guide that walks the centralized and decentralized setups.

The starting point is an awesome [guide](https://www.v7labs.com/blog/federated-learning-guide) awesome v7labs centralized decentralized etc: a step-by-step guide to federated learning in computer vision that explores the idea from the ground up, including its most common applications in machine learning.
